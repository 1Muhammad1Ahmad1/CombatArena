HOW TO DOWNLOAD AND PLAY COMBATARENA (Windows)
==============================================

Do NOT use the green "Code -> Download ZIP" button. That only gives you the source code,
not the game.

1. Download the game here (about 500 MB):

   https://github.com/1Muhammad1Ahmad1/CombatArena/releases/download/v1.0/CombatArena.exe

   (Release page: https://github.com/1Muhammad1Ahmad1/CombatArena/releases/tag/v1.0)

2. Double-click CombatArena.exe. No install, no account, works offline.

3. If Windows shows "Windows protected your PC", click "More info", then "Run anyway".
   (The game is not digitally signed, so Windows warns about it.)


CONTROLS
--------
W A S D        Move
Mouse          Look
Left mouse     Fire
Right mouse    Aim / sniper scope
Q              Switch weapon
R              Reload
Left Shift     Sprint
Space          Jump
C              Crouch (double-tap C while moving to slide)
Z              Prone
Esc            Pause
